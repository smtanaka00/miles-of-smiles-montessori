About Miles of Smiles Montessori Bulawayo

Where Every Discovery Begins with a Smile

At Miles of Smiles Montessori, we are dedicated to providing a nurturing, world-class early childhood education right in the heart of Bulawayo. We believe that a child's early years are a time of immense wonder, and our mission is to protect and cultivate that natural curiosity through the proven Montessori method.

Our Philosophy

We move beyond traditional "daycare" to provide a true prepared environment. Following the principles of Dr. Maria Montessori, our classrooms are designed to encourage independence, self-discipline, and a lifelong love for learning. Here, children are not just students—they are young explorers who are given the freedom to choose, the tools to succeed, and the space to grow at their own unique pace.

Why Miles of Smiles?

  • Authentic Montessori Learning: From practical life skills and sensorial exploration to early literacy and mathematics, our curriculum is hands-on and child-centered.
  • A Holistic Approach: We focus on the "whole child," nurturing their emotional, social, and physical well-being alongside academic milestones.
  • A Joyful Community: Our name reflects our daily reality. We pride ourselves on creating a warm, safe, and cheerful atmosphere where every child feels seen, respected, and loved.
  • Dedicated Educators: Our team is passionate about guiding young minds, ensuring that each "Miles of Smiles" student develops the confidence to navigate the world with grace and courtesy.

Join Our Family

We invite you to visit our campus in Bulawayo to experience the magic of a Montessori education firsthand. At Miles of Smiles, we aren't just preparing children for school; we are preparing them for life.
